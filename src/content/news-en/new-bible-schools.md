In 2024 we started a training school for prospective spiritual leaders in eastern Uganda. These leaders are being equipped to teach the fundamentals of the Christian faith to the enormous stream of converts, which has now grown to more than 20,000. The first school has 125 students who entered their second year in 2025 — a year of deepening in which they continue to grow in Bible knowledge and in walking with the Holy Spirit. Meanwhile, a second school with another 125 participants also started in January. Everything is aimed at equipping as many leaders as possible. The course material has largely been translated into the local Ugandan language and is taught by three dedicated pastors — two of whom lead a church on site. The demand for Bibles is enormous and grows by the day.
