In early November a very festive event took place: the graduation of no fewer than 65 students who completed the full-time programme. During the same gathering the 15th anniversary of the School for Harvest was also celebrated – an impressive milestone that shows how God has blessed this school over the years.

The impact of the school is clearly visible. In recent years more than 100 churches have been planted by former students and by teams sent out from the school. The many testimonies we were able to hear from former students gave a powerful and moving picture of what God has done through their lives.

Bert and Vincent had the privilege, by God’s grace, of being present at this special celebration. It was a moment of gratitude, connection and hope for the future.
