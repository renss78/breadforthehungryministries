At this moment there are 50,000 registered converts in the age group 18 to 92 years and 33,000 in the age group 5 to 17 years. In January 2026 we are starting a complete children’s ministry (Kingdom Kids) for all seven churches, to root this target group too in the love of God. The loudest cry that is heard from all sides at this moment is: **‘Give us a Bible’!**

When we first came, there was only 1 Bible in the entire area, which had been torn into various books so that everyone had a piece of the Bible. By God’s grace we have been able to build the natural infrastructure so that there are ‘channels’ through which the living water can flow. This has resulted in seven healthy churches surrounded by flourishing, evangelising house groups, two Bible schools where 250 students are trained every year so that the ministry team can expand structurally, and food production on a small scale.

The project now has a legal government status in Uganda, so that continuity is secured. The government is extremely enthusiastic and amazed at how all this is possible. The hunger and thirst is and remains truly overwhelming. We see with our own eyes the book of Acts being fulfilled, and we realise that it is all too big for us because of the speed at which the harvest is pouring in.
