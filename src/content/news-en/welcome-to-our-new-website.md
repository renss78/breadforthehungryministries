Welcome to our renewed website, and we are glad that you are here! This website is more than a digital place; it is an invitation to make a difference together.

Our mission? To make Jesus visible in word and deed, driven by God’s love. We offer Bible teaching through schools, seminars and conferences in, among other places, Kenya, Uganda, the Caribbean and Suriname. In addition, we support disadvantaged communities with agricultural projects and water wells. In this way we help people build a hopeful, self-sufficient future. Join us and make a difference – locally and worldwide.
