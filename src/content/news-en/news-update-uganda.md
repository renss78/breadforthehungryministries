At this moment there are 68,000 registered converts. In January 2026 we are starting the baptism of more than 60,000 believers within the seven new churches that were planted in the past year.

In addition, 10,000 Bibles are being distributed among the believers. This is done with great care: one Bible is shared by three families, so that as many people as possible have access to the Word.

In February 2026 we are travelling with a team of seven people to the revival area. There we will train the local leaders and help them develop a complete children’s ministry for all seven churches.

The theme of this training week is: **The Mystery of the Church Revealed** .
