On Monday 23 November Vincent, Mark, Nancy and I will fly to northern Kenya, close to the border with Sudan. As most of you know, in January we started a new Bible school there under the leadership of Pastor John, one of the first students at our school in Webuye, Kenya, which we founded more than 15 years ago. Pastor John has planted more than 20 churches in both Sudan and northern Kenya (a desert region).

He has invited us to a large pastors’ conference in northern Kenya and to the graduation of the 50 students, some of whom will go on to plant new churches, including in Sudan. In the evenings, evangelism and healing services will be held.

The journey there is rather complex because the region is remote and the flight connections are not well coordinated with one another.

Will you pray for this trip, for health, protection, impact and the home front
