Waiting in Entebbe for our flight at 00:40, we hear the first testimonies from yesterday. There are now 240 converts and testimonies of hundreds of healings.

 In addition, more than 500 people were baptised yesterday and today, something that will be in full swing over the coming month.

We are quiet and grateful for what happens when God’s Spirit is given room. In the revival area some 50 mosques are now empty. Muslims have found Jesus. How bitter to see that in our country mosques are being built while churches close. Oh Lord, visit us!
