Dear intercessors, a quick update and some footage from our mission trip.

All the services are soaked up by a diversity of nationalities. It is moving to be allowed to minister to the nations in all their diversity.
By now there are already around 100 new converts, and there is an astonishingly large number of young people. The workers of the last hour are being encouraged!
A youth group from Sudan sings ‘Africa will be saved’.

Mark feels completely ‘at home’ with them.
All the services and seminars have a special colour and impact. God’s Spirit is working very powerfully.
Enjoy the photos and clips.
Please pray for our stamina: the heat is exhausting, the days are long and intensive, and the nights 🥵

We will keep you updated.
Warm blessings from all of us.
